# Illustrated room and deck

Built-in image_gen was used to interpret actual Alibi photographs in the
existing house illustration's ink/woodcut style. Original PNG outputs are kept
beside this file; 1800px WebP derivatives are in src/assets/art. These are
illustrations, not documentary photographs. No third-party artwork was added.

Final website assets:
- [Interior](../../../src/assets/art/inside-illustration.webp)
- [Deck](../../../src/assets/art/deck-illustration.webp)

Original outputs: `inside-master.png` and `deck-master.png` beside this file.
Both final derivatives are 1800px-wide WebP images. The initial selected
composition hung the photo rope over the deck. Following owner feedback, both
illustrations are now clear, and the moving carousel has its own separate hero.

## Interior

Use case: style-transfer. Transform reference image 1 (the actual Alibi Incline Public House interior photo) into an original, finely engraved contemporary woodcut / ink and cut-paper illustration in the exact visual family of reference image 2 (the website's house illustration). Reference 1 supplies architecture and perspective; reference 2 supplies drawing texture and limited inks ONLY. Preserve the recognizable chunky honey-colored timber trusses with black metal plates, charcoal industrial ventilation, dark booths, open long communal timber tables, stools, and windows. Wide landscape composition, roughly 2:1, full-bleed horizontal website section artwork. View from the dining room toward the room and bar: spectacular timber canopy in upper half, lovely open central floor and communal tables in lower half, carefully carved hatching, deep pine-green/charcoal ink, toasted amber wood, muted sage and warm linen paper highlights. Warm welcoming late-afternoon light. Make it beautiful and handcrafted, not photographic and not generic AI painting. Keep the mid-upper band relatively quiet so an actual animated rope of photos can hang across the website later. DO NOT draw any photo cards, rope, text, signage, logos, letters, watermarks, UI, frames or borders. No invented mountains or lake. No people. Retain enough negative visual space for a moving foreground element without leaving a blank half-image. This is a standalone finished scene asset, not a screenshot of a website.

## Deck

Use case: style-transfer. Transform reference image 1 (Alibi Incline's actual sunny outdoor deck and bar photo) into an original premium woodcut / ink and cut-paper illustration, in the SAME visual family as reference image 2 (existing website house art). Reference 1 supplies the real architecture: charcoal outdoor bar facade, triangular fabric shade sails, narrow steel support poles, honey-colored timber rails and high-top communal tables, metal stools and the Tahoe pines. Reference 2 supplies the engraving/hatching style and limited pine-green, charcoal, toasted amber, warm linen and sage inks, not its composition. Create a wide landscape horizontal scene roughly 2:1 for a full-bleed website chapter. Beautiful late-afternoon amber light under the sails, layered forest at left, bar at right, useful open view through the middle and lower scene. Hand-cut paper and delicate carved wood grain, natural organic edges in the drawing, confidently authored not photorealistic. The website will lay a REAL animated rope of hanging photographs across the scene in the middle, so do not draw any rope, lamps on wires, photographs, cards, typography, signage, logo, watermark, UI, borders or frame. No people. No invented lake or mountain backdrop. Make this inviting, richly detailed but restrained enough for the moving foreground photos; no blank half-image. Standalone finished art, not a website mockup.
